# Design rationale

Reference: [IBM Carbon typography](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/typography/style-strategies), [data tables](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/data-table/specifications).

The customer-facing MVP uses a compact layout, IBM Plex Sans, neutral surfaces, a dark green action color, visible labels, predictable controls and accessible focus states. Fonts are local assets.

The primary screen contains a ticket input, sample selector, draft, classification, sources and reviewer actions. Source quotes and processing metadata expand on demand. Supporting screens contain the source corpus, interactive diagrams of the implemented workflow and proposed retrieval/review extensions, operational risks, saved evaluation results and a scenario calculator. Diagram nodes open concise explanations; planned mechanisms are labelled as plans. Implemented behavior and plans are labelled separately.

There are no slogans, editorial headings, Telegram links, research documents, decorative statistics or unsupported quality claims in the interface. The original research and attribution remain in repository documents.

On narrow screens navigation moves into a menu, panels stack, and wide tables scroll within their container. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
